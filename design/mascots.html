Notch Code — mascot concepts

Ten directions for a character that lives in the black island at the top of the screen. Every mascot's glowing bits follow the island's status colour — switch it below. Click a card to zoom.

Small previews (48 / 24 px) next to the swatches show how each reads at icon size.
01

Notchy the island itself

The notch comes alive: pill eyes, the voice bars become its mouth, little legs kicking below the screen edge. Most on-brand — the mascot is the product.

02

Bitwing the bat

Hangs upside-down from the top of your screen, just like the app. Bats hear everything — the sound arcs feed into its ears. Status colour lights its eyes.

03

Hoot the night owl

Wise, quiet, listens with headphones on. Belly feathers are little > prompt chevrons. Owls work alone at night — a nod to everything running locally.

04

Fen the fennec fox

Biggest ears in the animal kingdom, in Claude orange. Wears a black hoodie with the status dot on its chest. Friendly, warm, a little mischievous.

05

Jelly the jellyfish

Drifts calmly just below the island. Its tentacles are a live waveform, glowing in the status colour. Soft, ambient, never in the way.

06

Purr the notch cat

A black cat with a head shaped like the island, eyes glowing the status colour. The tail is a little sine wave. Sits quietly, notices everything.

07

Prompt the terminal bot

A tiny robot whose face is a terminal: > and a blinking _ for eyes, a waveform for a mouth. Leans into the "Code" half of the name.

08

Whisper the ghost

Named after whisper.cpp. A shy little ghost that whispers behind its hand — invisible, private, nothing leaves your machine. Pale against the dark UI.

09

Slo the sloth

Hangs from the island all day, perfectly relaxed. Sloths save energy — so does a local model on your own GPU. Status light glows on its belly.

10

Echo the parrot

Repeats everything you say — word for word. That's transcription. Perches on the island, Claude-orange feathers with an indigo tail, speech bubble typing the transcript.
